Formatting & pages

Font & sizeApplies to the whole selection — select a paragraph, several paragraphs, or the entire document (Ctrl+A)
Size boxPick a preset (8–144) or type any size from 4 to 400; ▲▼ and the arrow keys step through the presets
No selectionThe chosen font or size applies to the next text you type
PagesText flows onto new pages automatically; pick A4, Letter or Continuous in the toolbar
Page breakCtrl+Enter starts a new page; press it again on the same line to remove the break
PrintingPage → Print / PDF (or Ctrl+P). Page breaks are honoured and paper always prints in light colours, so printed pages match what you see

Pasting

Keep formattingKeeps the source fonts, sizes and colours (scripts and junk markup are removed)
Match documentKeeps bold, italics, headings and lists, but drops fonts, sizes and colours
Text onlyPlain text, nothing else
After pastingA small bar appears near the text — switch style there without pasting again
